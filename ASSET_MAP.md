## Missing assets & how they were handled


- No brand film was supplied → the hero play button scrolls to About (override with `company.brandFilmUrl` in `src/content/site.ts`).
- No real project photography → Work cards are clear placeholders with editable copy.
- No verified client stats → Impact strip uses one real figure (`4` ventures) and `XX+` placeholders flagged in content.
- No real social / email → `hello@astranova.example` and `#` social links are placeholders in content.
- Favicon/app-icon sizes were derived from the Aurora A symbol and the supplied dark app icon to match the brief's size list.
